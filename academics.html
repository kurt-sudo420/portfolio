Certification pathway

A deliberate sequence rather than a scattered list — networking fundamentals first, then the defensive baseline, then detection and response, finishing with hands-on offensive certification. Each one builds on the last.

  1. 01

    CompTIA Network+

    Networking foundations

    Topologies, protocols, addressing, and structured troubleshooting — the layer everything else sits on top of.

    Exam scheduled Sept 2026
  2. 02

    CompTIA Security+

    Defensive baseline

    Threats, risk management, cryptography, and controls — the vocabulary and frameworks the rest of the field assumes.

    In progress
  3. 03

    CompTIA CySA+

    Detection & response

    Behavioral analytics, threat hunting, and incident response — the certification that maps most directly onto my SOC shifts.

    In progress
  4. 04

    OSCP

    Offensive security

    Offensive Security Certified Professional — exploitation, privilege escalation, and a 24-hour hands-on practical exam. The long-term target.

    In progress
