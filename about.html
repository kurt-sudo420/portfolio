The short version

How I got here

I started out keeping a retail store's network and point-of-sale systems alive — unglamorous work, but it taught me to isolate a failure to hardware, network, or software before touching anything, which turns out to be most of what incident triage is.

That led to the university SOC, where I now spend my shifts in the alert queue: investigating phishing reports, running vulnerability scans across campus endpoints, and writing up findings that someone more senior has to be able to act on.

In parallel I picked up a Machine Psychology Fellowship with the Cyberpunk Division — the security arm of UMass Boston's Machine Psychology Group — red-teaming large language models. It's the same instinct pointed at a newer target — find out what a system does when you push it somewhere its designers didn't intend.

The through line is that I'd rather understand a thing by taking it apart than by reading about it, which is also why there's a rack of equipment in my house and a Flipper Zero on my desk.
